## Full script (to read straight through)

Clothing brand owners… what if you only paid for your ads when they actually made you a sale?
Right now, most agencies charge you every month. Sales or no sales.
We flipped that. I'm Ryan, I run Southline. We run Meta ads for clothing brands, and you pay us per sale.
No sale… no fee.
Here's how. We launch several ads, not one. Real buyers pick the winner. The budget moves to what's selling. And anyone who looked but didn't buy sees you again.
It all runs in your ad account, so you see every sale.
On top of that, we can set up the emails that bring them back for a second order.
Want your ads to pay for themselves? Comment SALES, or send me a DM, and I'll show you how it'd work for your brand.

## Three other hooks to test (swap the first line only)

1. "Your ad agency gets paid whether you sell anything or not. Ours doesn't."
2. "The question every clothing brand should ask an agency: what happens if I don't make a sale?"
3. "If your ads don't make a sale, why are you still paying for them?"

Run the main version and one alternative in the same ad set, everything else identical, and keep whichever has the cheaper cost per comment or DM after 4–5 days.

## Before you post

- **Ad spend:** "No sale, no fee" means *our* fee. If the brand pays the ad spend itself (it runs in their account), say so on the call so nobody feels misled.
- **Define "a sale":** decide how sales are counted (e.g. orders tracked from your ads in Meta Ads Manager) and put it in the agreement.
- **Comment SALES** needs a comment-to-DM automation set up for that word.
