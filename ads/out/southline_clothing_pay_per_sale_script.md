# Video ad script: "Does your agency get paid either way?" (clothing brand owners)

**Length:** about 30 seconds · 9:16 · Ryan to camera, with on-screen text
**Angle:** most agencies charge a monthly retainer whatever happens; Southline's fee is tied to the sales its ads bring in.
**Offer wording:** no monthly retainer, *our fee* is based on results. Never say or imply the brand "only pays per sale" or pays nothing otherwise: they still fund their own ad spend. Ad spend isn't mentioned in the ad; it's explained on the call.
**CTA:** comment or DM "SALES".

Read it like you're talking to one brand owner across a table: calm, sure, no hype. About 3 words a second.
"…" = a short pause. **Bold** = lean on that word.

## Full script (to read straight through)

Clothing brand owners… does your ad agency get paid even when you don't make a sale?
Most do. Same invoice every month… good month or bad.
We do it differently. I'm Ryan, I run Southline. We run Meta ads for clothing brands, with no monthly retainer.
Our fee is tied to the sales we bring in. So we only win when you do.
Here's how. We launch several ads, not one. Real buyers pick the winner. The budget moves to what's selling. And anyone who looked but didn't buy sees you again.
It all runs in your ad account, so you see every number.
On top of that, we can set up the emails that bring customers back for a second order.
Want an agency that's paid on results? Comment SALES, or send me a DM, and I'll show you how it'd work for your brand.

## Before you post

- **On the call:** explain that the brand funds its own ad spend in its own account, and our fee is per result on top.
- **Define "a sale":** decide how sales are counted (e.g. orders tracked from your ads in Meta Ads Manager) and put it in the agreement.
- **Comment SALES** needs a comment-to-DM automation set up for that word.
