# Video ad script: "An open letter to e-com store owners" (Meta ads)

## Where the format comes from

Researched in the Meta Ad Library on 28 October 2026 (active ads aimed at e-com / Shopify store owners, US · UK · IE · AU). The ads that have been **running longest** are the best sign of what's working, because nobody keeps paying for an ad that loses money:

| Running since | Advertiser | Ad | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~May 2026 (about 6 months, still live) | ProfitMetrics + founder page (Frederik Boysen) | "An open letter to Shopify owners: if your ROAS is high but your bank account is bleeding… read this" | **Open letter.** Calls out one owner, names a painful contradiction, explains the real reason, soft CTA. Run from both the brand and the founder's own page. |
| ~June 2026 (about 5 months) | ScaleShot | "This AI agent will help you get your first 100 sales" | Specific promise |
| ~July 2026 (about 4 months) | AdScale | "Trusted by 7,000+ online stores" | Social proof |
| ~July 2026 (about 4 months) | Socialproof | "4x ROAS or you don't pay" | Guarantee (not usable for us) |
| ~Sept 2026 | The Danjumas | "Your product isn't the problem" | Reassurance hook |

The **open letter** is the clear winner: the longest-running, run from two pages, and it fits Southline (no made-up stats, no guarantee needed). Several newer agency ads this month push the same point the letter makes below: on Meta now, testing different creative is what moves results ("Creative is the lever now", "Patterns beat one video").

Snapshot of the long-running open letter: https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=973438881732409

## The script

**Length:** about 40 seconds (open letters run a little longer and still hold attention because they read like a personal note)
**Format:** the letter appears on screen as a note, line by line, while Ryan reads it. Each line highlights as it's read (karaoke style). Ryan in a small circle in the corner, or full screen to camera.
**CTA:** comment LETTER.

Read it slowly and plainly, like you're reading a note you actually wrote to one person. "…" = a short pause. **Bold** = lean on that word.

## Full script (to read straight through)

An open letter… to every e-com store owner who thinks Meta ads don't work for them.

Dear store owner,

You tried Meta ads. You spent a few hundred. You got some clicks, a handful of sales… and a lot of "is this even worth it?"

So you decided ads just don't work for your store.

I don't think that's true. I think you ran one ad, to one audience… and judged the whole thing on it.

Here's what actually works. Several ads for your best seller, each from a different angle. Real buyers decide the winner. The budget goes behind it. And the people who looked but didn't buy… see you again.

That's not a trick. It's just how Meta works now. It rewards the stores that test.

If that sounds like your store, comment LETTER… and I'll send you the first three ads I'd run for you.

Ryan, Southline.

## How to run it like the original

- Run it from **your own page (Ryan)** as well as Southline's, the same way ProfitMetrics runs theirs from the founder's page. An open letter lands harder coming from a person.
- Make 2–3 versions with different first lines and keep the one with the cheapest comment or DM after 4–5 days:
  1. "An open letter to e-com store owners who think Meta ads don't work for them."
  2. "An open letter to every store owner who switched their ads off after a bad month."
  3. "An open letter to e-com store owners spending on ads and not sure what it's doing."
- **Comment LETTER** needs a comment-to-DM automation set up for that word, and you need the "first three ads" ready to send when people ask.
