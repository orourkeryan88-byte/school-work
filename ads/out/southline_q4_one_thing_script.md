## Read straight through

Q4 can turn a good clothing brand into a million-pound brand… if you do one thing properly.

From Black Friday to Christmas, people buy more than at any other time of the year. But ads get more expensive too, because every brand is fighting for the same people.

And here's where most brands go wrong. They launch their new ads on Black Friday week. So they're testing at the most expensive time of the year… with no idea what works.

The one thing? Test now. Before it gets expensive.

Take your best seller. Make four or five different ads for it: a close-up, a try-on, a customer review, a gift idea. Run them for a week or two on a small budget.

By Black Friday, you know which one sells. You put the budget behind that one… so you're spending on a winner, not a guess.

And everyone who looks in November but doesn't buy? Show them that ad again in December, when they're buying gifts.

Test early, spend late. Save this for when you plan your Q4.

## Notes

- **No pitch, on purpose.** There's no mention of Southline's service, the fee or a call. People who find it useful will look at your page, and that's the trust you're building.
- **The hook says "can".** It doesn't promise millions or quote anyone's results, so it stays honest while still stopping the scroll. A softer alternative if you want one: "Q4 can make your whole year… or cost you a fortune. It comes down to one thing."
